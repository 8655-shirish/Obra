## Design Studies

`hero.jpg`, `drawing-room.jpg`, `kitchen.jpg`, `dining-room.jpg` and `joinery.jpg` are photoreal generated studies created through the existing Higgsfield account with Seedream 4.5. They do not depict documented properties or client projects. Generation stopped when the provider returned `grace_daily_limit_reached`; licensed media fills the remaining roles, without synthetic scene stand-ins. The attempted Seedance job was rejected before creation and is not represented as a delivered asset.

The local `photography/media-ledger.json` records source links or generation identifiers, prompts, dimensions and checksums. The central shot list is `src/components/templates/painter-eighteen/media.json`. JPEG derivatives and the silent MP4 are hosted locally; the website does not hotlink media.

## Verification Scope

File integrity, dimensions, media decoding, responsive geometry, motion preferences and controls are checked separately from visual acceptance. The implementing session cannot read image attachments, so no pixel-level visual acceptance is claimed. Photographic crops, overall art direction and the film loop still require a visual review before publication.
