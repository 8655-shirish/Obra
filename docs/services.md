# Contractor Types & Typical Services

Reference for onboarding, service-picker defaults, and agent copy grounding.

- **Source:** [CSLB Licensing Classifications](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/About_Us/Library/Licensing_Classifications/) (California Contractors State License Board), plus each individual classification detail page.
- **Captured:** 2026-08-17
- **How to read this:** *Official scope* paraphrases the California statutory/regulatory definition of what the classification is licensed to do. *Typical services* are the plain-language, consumer-facing offerings a contractor in that trade normally advertises — useful for website service sections. *Keywords* are search/copy hints.
- **Caution:** scope wording is California-specific. Licensing rules differ by state; never present these as legal advice or as a contractor's licensed scope without verifying the actual license.

## A — General Engineering Contractor

**Official scope.** Fixed works requiring specialized engineering knowledge and skill: irrigation, drainage, water supply and flood control, dams and hydroelectric projects, levees and reclamation, railroads, highways, streets and roads, tunnels, airports, sewers and treatment plants, bridges and overpasses, pipelines, refineries and industrial plants, powerhouses, mines, land leveling and earthmoving, excavating, grading, trenching, paving, and related concrete work.

**Typical services**
- Site development, mass grading and earthmoving
- Road, street and highway construction and rehabilitation
- Underground utility installation (water, sewer, storm drain)
- Trenching, shoring and excavation
- Bridges, culverts, retaining walls and other structures
- Sewer and wastewater system construction
- Drainage, flood control and erosion control works
- Pipeline and transmission-line construction
- Heavy concrete work for fixed works
- Public-works and municipal contracting

**Keywords:** heavy civil, site work, grading, underground utilities, public works, excavation

## B — General Building Contractor

**Official scope.** Construction of structures for the support, shelter and enclosure of persons, animals or property, where the work requires at least two unrelated building trades or crafts. May self-perform framing and carpentry; other trades require the matching classification or a licensed subcontractor.

**Typical services**
- New home and custom home construction
- Commercial and tenant-improvement build-outs
- Whole-house remodels and additions
- Kitchen and bathroom remodeling
- ADU (accessory dwelling unit) construction
- Structural repairs and foundation work
- Framing and carpentry
- Permit management and plan coordination
- Design-build project management
- General contracting and trade subcontractor coordination

**Keywords:** general contractor, new construction, home addition, remodel, ADU, design-build

## B-2 — Residential Remodeling Contractor

**Official scope.** Improvements to existing residential wood-frame structures where a single contract requires at least three unrelated trades. Excludes structural changes to load-bearing elements, and excludes fire protection (C-16), asbestos abatement (C-22) and well drilling (C-57) unless separately licensed or subcontracted. System-level electrical, mechanical and plumbing changes require the proper classification; only minor fixture-level alterations are allowed.

**Typical services**
- Multi-trade interior remodels
- Kitchen refresh (cabinets, counters, flooring, paint)
- Bathroom remodels with fixture replacement
- Flooring replacement and tiling
- Drywall, plastering and painting packages
- Finish carpentry and trim
- Insulation upgrades
- Siding and roof repair
- Light-fixture, fan, outlet and switch replacement
- Faucet, sink, toilet and tub replacement

**Keywords:** residential remodeling, home renovation, kitchen remodel, bathroom remodel, multi-trade

## C — Specialty Contractor (umbrella)

**Official scope.** A contractor whose work requires special skill and whose principal business uses specialized building trades or crafts. The C classification is the umbrella under which all individual C-# specialties below are issued; it also covers servicing or testing fire-extinguishing systems.

**Typical services.** See individual specialty classifications below.

**Keywords:** specialty contractor, trade contractor, subcontractor

## C-2 — Insulation and Acoustical Contractor

**Official scope.** Installs insulating media and acoustical materials — including firestopping and thermal, sound and fire-resistant systems — in walls, ceilings, floors, attics and mechanical systems.

**Typical services**
- Attic and wall insulation installation
- Blown-in, batt and spray-foam insulation
- Crawl space and floor insulation
- Acoustical ceiling and sound-attenuation systems
- Duct and pipe insulation
- Firestopping and fire-resistive assemblies
- Insulation removal and replacement
- Air-sealing and energy-efficiency upgrades
- Radiant barrier installation

**Keywords:** insulation, spray foam, attic insulation, soundproofing, energy efficiency

## C-4 — Boiler, Hot Water Heating and Steam Fitting Contractor

**Official scope.** Installs, services and repairs power boiler installations, hot-water heating systems and steam fitting — including fire-tube and water-tube boilers, low-pressure hot-water boilers, piping, fittings, valves, gauges, pumps, radiators, convectors, fuel tanks and burners.

**Typical services**
- Boiler installation and replacement
- Boiler service, tune-up and annual inspection
- Hydronic (hot-water) heating system installation
- Steam piping and steam-trap repair
- Radiator and convector installation
- Circulating pump replacement
- Burner and fuel-oil system service
- Valve, gauge and control replacement
- Emergency boiler repair
- Commercial boiler-room retrofits

**Keywords:** boiler repair, hydronic heating, steam fitting, radiators, boiler installation

## C-5 — Framing and Rough Carpentry Contractor

**Official scope.** Performs form work, framing and rough carpentry to construct framed structures; installs or repairs framing components including sub-flooring, siding, exterior stairs and railings, and related rough carpentry.

**Typical services**
- Wood and light-gauge steel framing
- Wall, floor and roof framing
- Sub-floor and sheathing installation
- Concrete form work
- Exterior stairs, decks and railings
- Siding installation
- Structural framing repair and dry-rot replacement
- Wall openings for doors and windows
- Patio covers and pergola structures

**Keywords:** framing contractor, rough carpentry, deck builder, sheathing, dry rot repair

## C-6 — Cabinet, Millwork and Finish Carpentry Contractor

**Official scope.** Fabricates and installs cabinets, cases, sashes, doors, trim, nonbearing partitions and other finish carpentry by cutting, surfacing, joining, gluing and fabricating wood or other products, and installs that millwork in structures.

**Typical services**
- Custom cabinet design and fabrication
- Kitchen and bath cabinet installation
- Built-in shelving, closets and entertainment centers
- Cabinet refacing and refinishing
- Interior and exterior door installation
- Baseboard, casing and crown molding
- Wainscoting and paneling
- Stair rails, treads and balustrades
- Countertop and cabinet hardware installation
- Nonbearing partition construction

**Keywords:** custom cabinets, millwork, finish carpentry, trim carpentry, built-ins

## C-7 — Low Voltage Systems Contractor

**Official scope.** Installs, services and maintains energy-limited communication and low-voltage systems not exceeding 91 volts — telephone, sound, cable TV, closed-circuit video, satellite antennas, instrumentation and temperature controls, and similar systems.

**Typical services**
- Structured network and data cabling
- Telephone and intercom systems
- Security camera and CCTV installation
- Access control and door-entry systems
- Audio/visual and distributed sound systems
- Home theater wiring
- Fire and burglar alarm low-voltage wiring
- Satellite and cable TV distribution
- Smart-home and automation controls
- Thermostat and control-system wiring

**Keywords:** low voltage, structured cabling, security cameras, access control, smart home

## C-8 — Concrete Contractor

**Official scope.** Forms, pours, places, finishes and installs mass, pavement, flat and other concrete work, and sets screeds for pavements or flatwork. Does not include plaster coatings or reinforcing-steel placement as a sole business.

**Typical services**
- Concrete driveways and aprons
- Patios, walkways and pool decks
- Foundations, footings and slabs
- Stamped, colored and decorative concrete
- Concrete stairs, curbs and gutters
- Retaining wall footings
- Concrete cutting, removal and replacement
- Crack repair, resurfacing and leveling
- Sealing and finishing
- Commercial flatwork and tilt-up slabs

**Keywords:** concrete contractor, driveway, stamped concrete, slab, foundation, flatwork

## C-9 — Drywall Contractor

**Official scope.** Lays out and installs gypsum wallboard and wallboard assemblies including nonstructural metal framing, and performs taping and texturing, including compounds applied to produce a smooth or textured surface.

**Typical services**
- Drywall hanging and installation
- Taping, mudding and finishing (Levels 1–5)
- Texture application and matching
- Metal stud and nonstructural framing
- Water-damage drywall replacement
- Popcorn/acoustic ceiling removal and retexture
- Patch and small-hole repair
- Fire-rated and moisture-resistant assemblies
- Soundproof drywall systems

**Keywords:** drywall, sheetrock, taping, texture, ceiling repair, drywall patch

## C-10 — Electrical Contractor

**Official scope.** Places, installs, erects or connects electrical wires, fixtures, appliances, apparatus, raceways, conduits, solar photovoltaic cells and any part thereof that generates, transmits, transforms or uses electrical energy in any form or for any purpose.

**Typical services**
- Panel upgrades and service changes
- Whole-home rewiring and circuit additions
- Outlet, switch and dimmer installation
- Interior and exterior lighting installation
- Recessed and landscape lighting
- Ceiling fan installation
- EV charger installation
- Generator and transfer-switch installation
- Solar PV electrical connection
- Electrical troubleshooting, code corrections and inspections

**Keywords:** electrician, panel upgrade, rewiring, EV charger, lighting installation, electrical repair

## C-11 — Elevator Contractor

**Official scope.** Fabricates, erects, installs and repairs elevators — including sheave beams, motors, cables, guides, cabs, counterweights, doors, automatic and manual controls, signal systems and all associated equipment for electrical, hydraulic and manual installations.

**Typical services**
- Passenger and freight elevator installation
- Hydraulic and traction elevator modernization
- Residential elevator and lift installation
- Wheelchair lifts and LULA elevators
- Dumbwaiter installation
- Preventive maintenance contracts
- Controller and door-operator upgrades
- Safety testing and code compliance
- Emergency elevator repair and entrapment response

**Keywords:** elevator installation, elevator modernization, lift, dumbwaiter, elevator maintenance

## C-12 — Earthwork and Paving Contractor

**Official scope.** Digs, moves and places earth so that cuts, fills, excavations, grades, trenches, backfill or incidental tunnels can be executed (including use of explosives), and mixes, fabricates and places paving materials.

**Typical services**
- Grading and site preparation
- Excavation and trenching
- Backfill and soil compaction
- Asphalt paving and overlays
- Driveway and parking lot paving
- Asphalt patching, crack sealing and seal coating
- Land clearing
- Drainage swales and grading corrections
- Base rock and subgrade preparation
- Demolition of paved surfaces

**Keywords:** grading, excavation, asphalt paving, parking lot, seal coating, site prep

## C-13 — Fencing Contractor

**Official scope.** Constructs, erects, alters or repairs all types of fences, corrals, runs, railings, cribs, game-court enclosures, guard rails, barriers, playground game equipment, backstops, posts, flagpoles and gates — excluding masonry walls.

**Typical services**
- Wood privacy fence installation
- Vinyl and composite fencing
- Chain-link fencing and enclosures
- Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing
- Automatic and manual gate installation
- Pool safety fencing
- Guard rails and barriers
- Fence repair and post replacement
- Sports court and backstop enclosures
- Flagpole installation

**Keywords:** fence installation, privacy fence, gates, chain link, fence repair

## C-15 — Flooring and Floor Covering Contractor

**Official scope.** Prepares surfaces for flooring and installs carpet, resilient sheet goods, resilient tile, wood flooring (including finishing and repair) and other established flooring materials — except ceramic tile.

**Typical services**

**Official scope.** Lays out, fabricates and installs all types of fire protection systems including associated equipment; excludes electrical alarm systems.

## C-20 — Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating and Air-Conditioning Contractor

**Official scope.** Fabricates, installs, maintains, services and repairs warm-air heating systems and water-heating heat pumps complete with warm-air appliances; ventilating systems with blowers and plenums; air-conditioning systems with the AC unit; and the ducts, registers, flues, controls and related equipment.

- Ventilation and exhaust systems

## C-21 — Building Moving/Demolition Contractor

**Official scope.** Raises, lowers, cribs, underpins, demolishes and moves or removes structures including their foundations. Does not include alterations, additions, repairs or rehabilitation of permanently retained portions of structures.

**Typical services**

- Foundation removal

- Cribbing and underpinning

## C-22 — Asbestos Abatement Contractor

**Official scope.** Performs abatement — containment, encapsulation or removal — and disposal of asbestos-containing construction materials. Registration with Cal/OSHA and strict regulatory compliance apply.

**Typical services**

- Encapsulation of asbestos materials

- Air-clearance monitoring coordination

**Keywords:** asbestos abatement, asbestos removal, popcorn ceiling removal, containment, encapsulation

## ASB — Asbestos Certification

**What it is.** Not a classification — a certification. A contractor may not perform asbestos-related work involving 100 sq ft or more of asbestos-containing material unless they hold the C-22 classification or the license qualifier passes the asbestos certification examination. Certification only allows abatement work within classifications the contractor already holds.

**Where it shows up on a contractor's site**
- Certified asbestos-related work within their trade
- Safe removal disclosures during remodel or demolition projects
- Compliance and worker-safety credibility content

**Keywords:** asbestos certified, ASB certification, asbestos-related work

## HAZ — Hazardous Substance Removal Certification

**What it is.** A certification, not a classification. Required before a contractor engages in removal or remedial action at listed hazardous waste sites. Only issued to contractors holding an A, B, C-12, C-21, C-36, C-57 or C-61/D-40 license, and requires passing the hazardous substance certification examination.

**Where it shows up on a contractor's site**
- Contaminated soil removal and remediation
- Underground storage tank removal
- Site remediation for listed hazardous waste sites
- Environmental compliance credentials

**Keywords:** HAZ certification, hazardous substance removal, site remediation, UST removal
